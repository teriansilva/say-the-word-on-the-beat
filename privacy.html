Privacy

Say the Word on Beat (saywordsonbeat.com) · last updated 24 September 2026

In short

  • No account and no ads. We never sell your data. Games you choose to make public are visible to everyone.
  • One anonymous session cookie keeps your games and uploads working.
  • Visit statistics only if you press Accept — you can change that below.
  • Everything runs on servers we operate ourselves in the European Union. Nothing is loaded from Google or any other third party.

Analytics

If you press Accept, we count visits with Umami, running on our own server (analytics.superstatus.io). It sets no cookies and stores no identifier on your device. It receives the page address, the referring page, your screen size, language and browser, and a few interface events (for example “suggestion closed”). Your IP address is used only for the moment it takes to work out a country and an anonymous visit ID that is reset every month — the address itself is not stored. Nothing is loaded or sent until you choose, and we filter out automated browsers as far as we can recognise them (this is a best guess: it can also skip a real visitor). Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not sell or rent personal data and do not share it with advertisers. Games you publish are visible to everyone. Anyone holding a game’s share link can open it, and signed-in gameplayce.io players can copy it into their own account there (gameplayce.io is run by the same operator; only the game is copied, nothing about you). Our servers are backed up as a whole, so deleted data can remain in backups until they are overwritten.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy (Art. 15, 20), correct it (Art. 16), delete it (Art. 17), restrict its use (Art. 18) or object to uses based on legitimate interest (Art. 21), and you can withdraw consent at any time with effect from then on (Art. 7(3)). Because there are no accounts, tell us which game or share link your request is about. Write to marcusbraun@live.com — we answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority — for us, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of the Free Hanseatic City of Bremen, or the authority where you live or work.
