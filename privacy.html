Privacy

Say the Word on Beat (saywordsonbeat.com) · last updated 26 September 2026

In short

  • No account and no ads. We never sell your data. Games you choose to make public are visible to everyone.
  • One anonymous session cookie keeps your games and uploads working.
  • We count visits with our own cookieless analytics — you can switch that off below.
  • Everything runs on servers we operate ourselves in the European Union. Nothing is loaded from Google or any other third party.

Analytics

We count visits with Umami, a self-hosted, cookieless analytics tool on our own server. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device (except remembering your choice if you switch analytics off), and does not keep your IP address. It records the page, referrer, browser, device type, screen size, language and country, plus a few interface events (for example when the Gameplayce suggestion is closed). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can switch it off at any time under Privacy settings, and we honour your browser’s Global Privacy Control signal. We also filter out automated browsers as far as we can recognise them.

We do not sell or rent personal data and do not share it with advertisers. Games you publish are visible to everyone. Anyone holding a game’s share link can open it, and signed-in gameplayce.io players can copy it into their own account there (gameplayce.io is run by the same operator; only the game is copied, nothing about you). Our servers are backed up as a whole, so deleted data can remain in backups until they are overwritten.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy (Art. 15, 20), correct it (Art. 16), delete it (Art. 17), restrict its use (Art. 18) or object to uses based on legitimate interest (Art. 21), and you can switch analytics off at any time under Privacy settings. Because there are no accounts, tell us which game or share link your request is about. Write to marcusbraun@live.com — we answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority — for us, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of the Free Hanseatic City of Bremen, or the authority where you live or work.
